About Sage Lawn and Landscape, LLC

Owner-operated outdoor construction for Kansas homeowners who want practical, lasting improvements.

Our approach

Local experience with direct communication

Sage Lawn and Landscape, LLC is a locally based company serving Topeka, Lawrence, and surrounding communities. The business grew from more than 15 years of hands-on residential landscaping experience and a focus on doing the work correctly from the beginning.

The owner remains involved throughout the project. That means site questions, material choices, scheduling updates, and the final walkthrough stay connected instead of being passed between different people.

What we build

Permanent outdoor improvements

Fences

Privacy fencing designed around your property, grade, gates, and material preferences.

Decks

Wood and composite decks planned for the way your family uses the backyard.

Hardscapes

Paver patios, walkways, fire-pit areas, and outdoor gathering spaces with proper base preparation.

Retaining Walls

Structural landscape features that address slopes, erosion, drainage, and usable yard space.

Turf & Putting Greens

Low-maintenance synthetic surfaces for lawns, pets, and recreational areas.

Site Improvements

Regrading, sod, concrete patios, pergolas, gazebos, and other outdoor improvements when appropriate.

Experience

Built around real Kansas site conditions

Kansas weather, freeze-thaw cycles, clay soils, drainage patterns, slopes, and access can all affect an outdoor project. Our planning considers those conditions before construction begins.

Site evaluation

We look at the property before recommending a solution. Grade, drainage, soil, existing structures, access, and intended use all matter.

Workmanship

We focus on the details that affect durability, including proper post setting, footing preparation, compaction, drainage, and suitable materials.
