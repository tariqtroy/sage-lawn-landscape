Landscaping & Outdoor Construction Services

Practical outdoor improvements for properties in Topeka, Lawrence, and surrounding Kansas communities.

01

Privacy Fence Installation

We build wood privacy fences using cedar and pressure-treated lumber. Planning includes the fence line, gates, grade changes, property considerations, and underground utility marking before digging. The goal is a straight, secure fence that fits the property and holds up through Kansas seasons.

02

Custom Deck Construction

Ground-level and raised decks can be built in wood or composite materials. We handle practical layouts, stairs, railings, framing, and footings while considering the site and intended use. A good deck starts below the visible surface, so structural preparation receives the same attention as the finished appearance.

03

Paver Patios & Hardscapes

Paver patios, walkways, fire-pit areas, and outdoor seating spaces depend on the base underneath them. We focus on excavation, crushed-stone preparation, compaction, drainage, and joint sand so the finished surface has the support it needs to stay stable.

04

Retaining Wall Installation

Retaining walls can help manage slopes, erosion, drainage, and difficult grades. Depending on the property, options may include segmental concrete systems, natural stone, or other suitable construction methods. Proper base preparation and drainage behind the wall are essential to long-term performance.

05

Artificial Turf & Putting Greens

Artificial turf can provide a low-maintenance outdoor surface for lawns, pets, and recreational areas. We also install backyard putting greens for homeowners who want a dedicated practice space with a consistent appearance.

06

Additional Outdoor Improvements

Depending on the project, we may also handle sod installation, yard regrading, concrete patios, pergolas, gazebos, flower-bed restoration, and building pads. If your project involves a larger outdoor improvement, contact us to discuss the site and scope.

Our process

2. Project Plan
